📌 Server Guidelines 📌

Please read and follow these rules to maintain a safe and fun community!

01

Respect Everyone ໒꒱˚

Treat all members normally, with no favoritism and with full respect—especially when they are doing nothing to harm or bother others.

02

Complaining & Support 🎟️

If you have a problem or complaint, open a ticket to report any private complaints or concerns directly to the staff team.

03

No Advertising 🚫

No advertising other Discord servers, Roblox games, or personal social media accounts without explicit staff permission.

04

Protect Privacy 🔒

Never share your own or another person's private information without their permission.

05

Be Honest ✨

Always maintain honesty across the community. Never lie or share fake information.

06

No False Reports ⚖️

Do not submit false evidence or make knowingly false accusations against any player or staff member.
